# EduVision – KPI Definitions

## 1. Overview

This document defines the major KPIs used in the EduVision higher education dashboards.

---

## 2. University KPIs

### Total Universities

Represents the number of universities available in the selected dataset or filter context.

### Average Overall Score

Represents the average overall performance score of the selected universities.

### Average QS Score

Represents the average QS-related performance score of the selected universities.

### Global Ranking Score

Represents the ranking-related performance indicator used to compare universities.

### Academic Reputation Score

Represents the academic reputation performance of universities based on the available dataset.

---

## 3. Research KPIs

### Research Impact

Represents the research impact performance of a university.

### Research Output Score

Represents the research output performance available in the dataset.

### Citation Impact Score

Represents the citation-related research performance of a university.

---

## 4. Student KPIs

### Student Population

Represents the number of students associated with a university.

### International Student Percentage

Represents the percentage of students who are international students.

### International Student Ratio

Represents the proportion or ratio of international students used for comparison across universities or countries.

### Faculty-to-Student Ratio

Represents the relationship between faculty members and students.

### Students-to-Staff Ratio

Represents the number of students relative to staff.

---

## 5. Country KPIs

### Country Average Rank

Represents the average university ranking value for a country.

### Country Average Score

Represents the average education performance score for universities associated with a country.

### Best University Rank

Represents the best available university ranking associated with a country.

---

## 6. KPI Usage

These KPIs are used throughout the EduVision dashboards to:

- Compare universities.
- Compare countries.
- Analyze research performance.
- Analyze student characteristics.
- Identify high-performing regions.
- Support interactive dashboard analysis.
